# Moving-In Condition Report & Rights Checklist — Queensland
### Get this right on day one — it protects your bond for the entire tenancy.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal advice.*

---

## Section 1 — Before You Sign the Lease

- [ ] **Confirm the property meets Queensland's Minimum Housing Standards.** Since 1 September 2024, all rental properties must be: weatherproof, structurally sound, and free from mould and vermin; have functioning locks, adequate plumbing, and privacy window coverings; and provide safe fixtures and fittings, plus a functional kitchen and laundry where these are supplied.
- [ ] **Know your right to end a tenancy if standards aren't met** — you can raise this as grounds to challenge or end the agreement if the property genuinely fails these standards.
- [ ] **Ask about smoke alarm compliance** and any recent safety checks.
- [ ] **If you have a pet, submit your request in writing** — the landlord must respond within **14 days** or consent is deemed granted, and refusal must be on reasonable grounds.

## Section 2 — The Condition Report Itself

- [ ] **Request the entry condition report (Form 1a)** if not automatically provided.
- [ ] **Complete your own section thoroughly**, room by room.
- [ ] **Photograph everything**, especially existing wear, damage or imperfections.
- [ ] **Note existing issues explicitly.**
- [ ] **Test everything that should work**: taps, toilets, switches, power points, stovetop, and any included appliances.
- [ ] **Submit your condition report within the required timeframe.**
- [ ] **Keep a copy for yourself** — you'll need it again at the end of your lease alongside the Form 14a exit report.

## Section 3 — Ongoing Rights During Your Tenancy

- [ ] **Know your rent increase protections.** Rent can be increased **only once every 12 months**, and since 6 June 2024, this limit applies to the **property itself**, not just your specific tenancy — meaning a landlord can't reset the clock by ending your lease and re-letting at a higher price.
- [ ] **Know you can challenge an excessive rent increase.** QCAT can assess whether an increase is excessive compared to market rates and other relevant factors.
- [ ] **Know that no-grounds evictions have been banned since 1 October 2022** — Queensland was actually ahead of other states here. Landlords must provide a valid reason: sale requiring vacant possession, significant renovation, the owner or family member moving in, or a genuine breach.
- [ ] **Know your protection if a household member is violent.** Since 6 June 2024, co-renters can apply to QCAT to be recognised as the sole renter in this circumstance, protecting their tenancy independently.
- [ ] **Request repairs in writing** — this creates a paper trail if you later need a QCAT repair order.

## Section 4 — Red Flags at Move-In

- [ ] **A condition report with suspiciously few noted issues** on an older or clearly lived-in property.
- [ ] **Being pressured to sign before receiving the condition report or properly inspecting.**
- [ ] **Signs the property fails Minimum Housing Standards** — non-functioning locks, missing window coverings, visible mould, or an unsafe kitchen/laundry.
- [ ] **A blanket "no pets" refusal without a stated reasonable ground.**
