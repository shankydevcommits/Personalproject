# Rental Application Fast-Track Checklist — Queensland
### Win the lease, even with no rental history or connections.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal advice.*

---

## Section 1 — Know Your Rights Before You Apply

- [ ] **Understand the standardised application form.** Since May 2025, Queensland requires the use of a **standardised rental application form (Form 22)**, with new rules limiting what personal information can be collected, how it's stored, and how it must be destroyed.
- [ ] **Know your data is protected.** Agents and landlords can't request excessive personal information beyond what the prescribed form requires, and must handle what they do collect in line with the new privacy rules.
- [ ] **Know Queensland's tenancy law is administered under the Residential Tenancies and Rooming Accommodation Regulation 2025**, with the **Residential Tenancies Authority (RTA)** holding bonds and providing dispute resolution, and the **Queensland Civil and Administrative Tribunal (QCAT)** hearing disputes.

## Section 2 — Documents to Have Ready

- [ ] **Photo ID** (driver's licence or passport).
- [ ] **Proof of income** — payslips, employment letter, or for students/first-time renters: Centrelink statements, scholarship confirmation, or guarantor documents.
- [ ] **Rental history/references** — previous landlord/agent details, or alternative references if you're a first-time renter.
- [ ] **Bank statements**, if requested and permitted under Form 22's scope.

## Section 3 — The Application Itself

- [ ] **Apply promptly after inspecting** — competitive Brisbane and South East Queensland properties can attract high application volumes quickly.
- [ ] **Only provide information the Form 22 actually requests** — you're entitled to decline requests for anything beyond this.
- [ ] **Attend the inspection in person if possible.**
- [ ] **Follow up politely, once**, if you haven't heard back within a few days.

## Section 4 — Renting With Pets

- [ ] **Submit pet requests in writing.** Landlords must respond within **14 days**, or consent is **deemed granted** if they don't.
- [ ] **Know refusals must be on "reasonable grounds"** — a blanket no-pets policy without a stated, reasonable reason isn't compliant.

## Section 5 — For Students & First-Time Renters Specifically

- [ ] **Consider a guarantor** if you lack independent rental/income history — confirm the specific agency genuinely accepts this.
- [ ] **Highlight stability signals** — enrolment confirmation, course length, stable casual work history.
- [ ] **Consider co-applying with reliable housemates** for a stronger combined application.
- [ ] **Don't be discouraged by rejections** in a competitive market — apply for multiple properties in parallel.
