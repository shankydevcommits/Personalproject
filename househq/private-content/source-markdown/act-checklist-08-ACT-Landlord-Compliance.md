# Landlord Compliance Checklist — ACT
### The obligations every rental provider must meet under the Residential Tenancies Act 1997.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal advice. Confirm current requirements with Access Canberra before relying on this checklist alone.*

---

## Section 1 — Bonds

- [ ] **Respect the maximum bond of 4 weeks' rent.**
- [ ] **Lodge the bond with the ACT Revenue Office** — within **2 weeks** if you're a private lessor, within **4 weeks** if you're using an agent.
- [ ] **Provide a receipt as proof of bond payment.**
- [ ] **Never claim fair wear and tear against a bond.**

## Section 2 — Rent

- [ ] **Provide at least 8 weeks' written notice** for any rent increase.
- [ ] **Never increase rent within the first 12 months** of a tenancy agreement.
- [ ] **Know tenants can dispute an increase they consider excessive**, potentially through ACAT.

## Section 3 — Condition Reports and Repairs

- [ ] **Complete entry and exit condition reports** for every tenancy.
- [ ] **Respond to repair requests promptly**, particularly heating-related issues given Canberra's climate — this is a genuinely higher-stakes habitability item here than in warmer states.
- [ ] **Keep thorough records** — photos, correspondence, receipts, and condition reports.

## Section 4 — Tax and Financial Obligations

- [ ] **Understand you'll pay land tax on any ACT rental property**, since it's not your principal place of residence — this is separate from rates, which apply to all ACT properties.
- [ ] **Check your foreign ownership surcharge exposure** if applicable — a 7% surcharge applies to foreign owners.
- [ ] **Keep records for your tax depreciation schedule and annual land tax assessments.**

## Section 5 — Disputes

- [ ] **Understand ACAT's role** in resolving tenancy disputes — bonds, rent arrears, property damage claims, and breach matters all fall within its jurisdiction.
- [ ] **Keep thorough documentation** for any matter that could end up before the Tribunal — condition reports, correspondence, and receipts are what determines most outcomes.

## Quick Reference — ACT Landlord Compliance Calendar

| Obligation | Detail |
|---|---|
| Maximum bond | 4 weeks' rent |
| Bond lodgement | 2 weeks (lessor) / 4 weeks (agent) |
| Rent increase notice | 8 weeks, none in first 12 months |
| Land tax | Applies to non-PPR rental properties |
| Dispute resolution | ACAT |
