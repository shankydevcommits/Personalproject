# Pre-Settlement to Moving Day Checklist — Western Australia
### The logistics, from signing (which is binding immediately) to move-in.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal or financial advice.*

---

## Section 1 — Immediately After Signing

- [ ] **Remember you're already bound** — there's no cooling-off period in WA, so from the moment of signing, move straight into settlement preparation rather than treating the early days as a reconsideration window.
- [ ] **Confirm building insurance timing with your settlement agent** and arrange cover promptly.
- [ ] **Send your signed contract to your lender/broker immediately.**
- [ ] **Engage your settlement agent or solicitor formally** to begin searches and liaison with the seller's side.

## Section 2 — 4–6 Weeks Before Settlement

- [ ] **Finalise unconditional loan approval.**
- [ ] **Get at least 3 removalist quotes** and book early.
- [ ] **Start decluttering and sourcing packing materials.**
- [ ] **Notify your current landlord/agent (if renting)** of your move-out date, respecting your required notice.

## Section 3 — 1–2 Weeks Before Settlement

- [ ] **Organise utility connections** — electricity, gas, water, internet/NBN.
- [ ] **Arrange disconnection at your current address** for the day after you vacate.
- [ ] **Redirect your mail** via Australia Post.
- [ ] **Update your address** with: the electoral roll, your bank(s), employer, Medicare, insurer(s), and the Department of Transport (driver's licence and vehicle registration).
- [ ] **Confirm final settlement figures**, including rates and strata levy adjustments if applicable.
- [ ] **Lodge your FHOG application with your home loan application** if you haven't already — this is the recommended timing in WA rather than waiting until after settlement.

## Section 4 — Settlement Week

- [ ] **Book your pre-settlement inspection** to confirm the property matches the agreed condition and inclusions.
- [ ] **Confirm final funds transfer arrangements** with your settlement agent and lender.
- [ ] **Confirm key collection arrangements.**
- [ ] **Confirm your removalist booking for the day after settlement** (or settlement day if timing allows).

## Section 5 — Moving Day and Just After

- [ ] **Do a final walkthrough before unpacking**, photographing anything not present at your pre-settlement inspection.
- [ ] **Locate your fuse box, water mains shut-off, and gas shut-off** early.
- [ ] **Set up home and contents insurance** if not already arranged.
- [ ] **Register relevant council services** if not automatically transferred.
- [ ] **Keep all settlement paperwork** together — contract, loan documents, FHOG confirmation.

---

## Quick Reference — Settlement Week Timeline

| Timing | Key task |
|---|---|
| Signing (immediately binding) | Confirm insurance timing, engage settlement agent |
| 4–6 weeks out | Loan approval finalised, book removalist |
| 1–2 weeks out | Utilities connected, mail redirected, address updates |
| Settlement week | Pre-settlement inspection, confirm funds transfer |
| Settlement day | Keys released, final walkthrough |
