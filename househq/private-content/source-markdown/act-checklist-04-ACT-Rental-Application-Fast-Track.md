# Rental Application Fast-Track Checklist — ACT
### Win the lease, even with no rental history or connections.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal advice.*

---

## Section 1 — Know Your Rights Before You Apply

- [ ] **Know your tenancy is governed by the Residential Tenancies Act 1997 (ACT)**, with the ACT Civil and Administrative Tribunal (ACAT) handling disputes.
- [ ] **Know your rent increase protections apply from the very start of your tenancy** — increases can't occur within the first 12 months, so a fresh lease gives you at least a year of price certainty.

## Section 2 — Documents to Have Ready

- [ ] **Photo ID** (driver's licence or passport).
- [ ] **Proof of income** — payslips, employment letter, or for students/first-time renters: Centrelink statements, scholarship confirmation, or guarantor documents.
- [ ] **Rental history/references** — previous landlord/agent details, or alternative references if you're a first-time renter.
- [ ] **Bank statements**, if requested.

## Section 3 — The Application Itself

- [ ] **Apply promptly after inspecting** — Canberra's rental market remains competitive for well-located properties.
- [ ] **Attend the inspection in person if possible.**
- [ ] **Follow up politely, once**, if you haven't heard back within a few days.

## Section 4 — For Students & First-Time Renters Specifically

- [ ] **Consider a guarantor** if you lack independent rental/income history — Canberra's significant student and public-service-transfer population means agencies are often reasonably familiar with these situations, but confirm the specific agency accepts it.
- [ ] **Highlight stability signals** — enrolment confirmation, course length, stable casual or public sector work history.
- [ ] **Consider co-applying with reliable housemates** for a stronger combined application.
- [ ] **Don't be discouraged by rejections** — apply for multiple properties in parallel.

## Section 5 — Know Your Ongoing Protections Once You're In

- [ ] **Know your bond is capped at 4 weeks' rent**, and must be lodged with the ACT Revenue Office.
- [ ] **Know rent increase rules**: at least **8 weeks' written notice**, and no increase within the first 12 months of your tenancy.
- [ ] **Know disputes go to ACAT**, not a general court — a specialist tribunal for tenancy matters.
