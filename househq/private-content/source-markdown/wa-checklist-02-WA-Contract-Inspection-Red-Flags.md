# Contract & Building/Pest Inspection Red Flags — Western Australia
### What to check before you sign — because in WA, there's no cooling off to fall back on.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal advice — always have a settlement agent or solicitor review your specific contract before signing.*

---

## Section 1 — The Single Most Important Thing to Understand About WA Contracts

- [ ] **There is no cooling-off period in Western Australia.** Once both parties sign the contract of sale, you are bound — there is no statutory right to withdraw, whether the sale is private treaty or auction. This makes pre-signature diligence far more critical in WA than in states with a cooling-off buffer.
- [ ] **Every condition you need must be written into the contract before you sign** — finance approval, building/pest inspection, sale of your existing property, or anything else. A verbal understanding or an intention to "sort it out later" has no legal protection once you've signed.

## Section 2 — Building & Pest Inspection: Even More Critical Here

- [ ] **Arrange your building and pest inspection before you sign**, or ensure the contract includes a clearly worded, time-limited inspection condition that lets you exit if the results are unsatisfactory.
- [ ] **Use a licensed, independent inspector** — not one exclusively recommended by the selling agent without your own due diligence.
- [ ] **Get both a building and pest inspection**, ideally combined.
- [ ] **Ask specifically about**: structural issues, rising damp, roof condition, asbestos (common in pre-1990s homes), and general electrical/plumbing age.
- [ ] **Get the report in writing before you sign**, not after — given there's no cooling-off period, a verbal "looks fine" from an inspector isn't something you can act on if the written report later reveals issues.

## Section 3 — Contract Fundamentals

- [ ] **Check the finance clause wording carefully** — if you're not fully pre-approved, ensure a genuine, clearly time-limited finance condition is included, since it's your only formal exit if finance falls through.
- [ ] **Confirm the settlement date works with your finance, insurance, and moving logistics.**
- [ ] **Confirm deposit terms** — amount, due date, and where it's held (typically in a settlement agent's or agent's trust account).
- [ ] **Confirm all expected inclusions are explicitly listed** in the contract, not just verbally promised.
- [ ] **If buying a strata property, review the strata records** — recent meeting minutes, financials, sinking fund balance, and any known disputes or special levies.

## Section 4 — Before You Sign: Final Checks

- [ ] **Have a settlement agent or solicitor review the entire contract before you sign it** — not after, and not as a formality. This is your primary protection given the absence of a cooling-off period.
- [ ] **Don't let time pressure push you into signing before your review and inspection are genuinely complete** — a rushed signature in WA is far riskier than in states where a cooling-off window exists as a backstop.
- [ ] **If you're at all uncertain, ask for more time before signing** — it's a reasonable request, and far cheaper than an unwanted, binding commitment.

## Quick Reference — Key WA Contract Realities

| Protection | What it means |
|---|---|
| Cooling-off period | **None** — you're bound the moment both parties sign |
| Finance/inspection conditions | Must be explicitly written into the contract before signing |
| Building/pest inspection | Not automatically provided — arrange your own before signing |
| Contract review | Must happen before signature, not after |
