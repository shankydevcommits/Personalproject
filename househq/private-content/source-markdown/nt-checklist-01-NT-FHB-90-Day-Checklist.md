# The First Home Buyer's 90-Day Checklist — Northern Territory
### Everything you need to sort before you sign — the NT does this very differently.

*Correct as at July 2026. Grant amounts and thresholds are set by government and can change — always confirm current details with the Territory Revenue Office (treasury.nt.gov.au) before relying on this for a purchase decision. This checklist is general information, not financial or legal advice.*

---

## Section 1 — Understand the NT's Very Different Approach (Grants, Not Duty Relief)

- [ ] **Know the NT takes a fundamentally different approach to most other states.** The Northern Territory offers **no general first home buyer stamp duty exemption or concession** — you'll pay standard transfer duty rates unless a specific exemption applies (see below). Instead, the NT compensates with **Australia's most generous cash grants**.
- [ ] **Check your eligibility for the HomeGrown Territory Grant.** A **$50,000** payment for eligible first home buyers **building or buying a new home**, with **no property price cap**, for contracts entered into before **30 September 2026** — confirm this deadline hasn't been extended or has closed by the time you're reading this.
- [ ] **Check the established-home grant separately.** A **$10,000** grant was available for first home buyers purchasing an established home, but only for contracts signed between 1 October 2024 and 30 September 2025 — **this window has likely closed as at mid-to-late 2026; confirm current availability of any established-home support with the Territory Revenue Office.**
- [ ] **Understand the House and Land Package Exemption (HLPE).** This gives a **full stamp duty waiver** for eligible house-and-land packages purchased under a single contract from a registered builder, for contracts signed between 1 July 2022 and 30 June 2027, with **no property value cap**. This is not restricted to first home buyers, but is especially valuable if you're building.
- [ ] **Understand the FreshStart New Home Grant is for existing homeowners, not first home buyers.** It provides $30,000 for existing owners purchasing or building a new home and isn't means-tested — relevant context if you're comparing NT schemes, but not something you'll access as a first home buyer.
- [ ] **Know you generally can't combine the HomeGrown Grant with the FreshStart Grant** — you can only receive one grant.
- [ ] **Check the federal First Home Guarantee** (5% deposit, no LMI) — confirm current NT price caps with a participating lender.
- [ ] **Get formal pre-approval, not just an estimate.**

## Section 2 — While You're Searching

- [ ] **Set a firm maximum price** based on your pre-approval — remember, unlike most states, there's no stamp duty saving cushioning an established-home purchase here.
- [ ] **Budget for full standard stamp duty on an established home purchase**, since the NT offers no general FHB duty concession — the NT's stamp duty uses a formula-based calculation for properties under $525,000, and tiered flat rates above that.
- [ ] **Consider whether building (via the HLPE) could be significantly more cost-effective than buying established**, given the potential full duty waiver.
- [ ] **Budget for costs beyond the purchase price**: conveyancing fees, building and pest inspections, loan fees, and moving costs.

## Section 3 — Know Your Legal Protections Before You Sign

- [ ] **Understand the cooling-off period.** The NT offers **4 business days** from the final signature/exchange to withdraw — and unlike most other states, this typically comes **with no financial penalty** for changing your mind.
- [ ] **Know both parties can agree to extend, reduce, or waive the cooling-off period** — the NT's system is notably flexible here compared to other states' fixed statutory approaches.
- [ ] **Get a building and pest inspection done within your cooling-off window.**

## Section 4 — Between Signing and Settlement

- [ ] **Confirm building insurance timing with your conveyancer.**
- [ ] **Finalise unconditional loan approval** well before settlement.
- [ ] **Book a pre-settlement inspection** in the days before settlement.
- [ ] **Confirm your grant application is genuinely lodged and processing** — given the HomeGrown Grant's 30 September 2026 contract deadline, timing genuinely matters if you're purchasing close to this date.

## Section 5 — Common First-Home-Buyer Mistakes to Avoid

- [ ] **Don't assume there's a stamp duty exemption like other states have** — the NT's support model is grant-based, not duty-based, for general established-home purchases.
- [ ] **Don't miss the HomeGrown Grant contract deadline** — 30 September 2026, confirm this hasn't changed before relying on it.
- [ ] **Don't overlook the HLPE if you're building** — a full duty waiver on a house-and-land package can be worth tens of thousands of dollars.

## Quick Reference — Northern Territory First Home Buyer Support (July 2026)

| Support | Amount / Detail | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| HomeGrown Territory Grant | $50,000, no price cap | New homes, contracts before 30 September 2026 |
| Established home grant | $10,000 | Contracts 1 Oct 2024–30 Sept 2025 — likely closed, confirm |
| House and Land Package Exemption (HLPE) | Full duty waiver, no cap | Single contract, registered builder, until 30 June 2027 |
| General FHB stamp duty exemption | **None** | NT is the only state/territory without one |
| Cooling-off period | 4 business days | Generally no penalty — flexible by agreement |

*Sources: Territory Revenue Office (treasury.nt.gov.au), NT.GOV.AU. Always verify current figures and deadlines before making a purchase decision.*
