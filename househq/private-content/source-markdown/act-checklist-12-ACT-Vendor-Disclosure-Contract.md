# Vendor Disclosure & Contract Checklist — ACT
### Your obligations as a seller.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal advice. Your contract should be prepared with a conveyancer or solicitor — this checklist helps you gather what they'll need, not replace their advice.*

---

## Section 1 — Understanding Your Disclosure Obligations

- [ ] **Know ACT sale contracts require disclosure documents to be attached before signing.** Your conveyancer or solicitor will confirm exactly what's required for your specific property, but this generally includes matters like title details, lease terms, and known encumbrances.
- [ ] **Understand you're selling leasehold, not freehold, title** — disclosure should accurately reflect the terms of the Crown lease.
- [ ] **Work with your conveyancer to prepare the contract properly before marketing begins.**

## Section 2 — Information to Gather for Your Conveyancer/Solicitor

- [ ] **Crown lease details and any encumbrances** — mortgages, easements, covenants affecting the property.
- [ ] **Zoning and planning information.**
- [ ] **Known building work history and any relevant approvals.**
- [ ] **Owners corporation records**, if applicable — recent meeting minutes, financials, sinking fund details, and known disputes or special levies.
- [ ] **Any known defects or issues** you're aware of, even if not immediately obvious — full disclosure protects you from post-settlement disputes.
- [ ] **Any historical asbestos assessments**, if relevant to your property's age and history.

## Section 3 — Understanding Your Buyer's New Financial Position

- [ ] **Understand many prospective buyers may now be eligible for the ACT's new stamp duty abolition** (first home buyers, or buyers of new unit-titled property) — this can affect buyer budgets and interest levels, and your agent's marketing strategy should account for this.

## Section 4 — Underquoting and Price Transparency

- [ ] **Discuss price guide practices with your agent** to ensure representations to prospective buyers are genuine and well-supported.
- [ ] **Don't ask your agent to advertise a misleadingly low price to generate interest.**

## Section 5 — Final Checks Before Going to Market

- [ ] **Confirm your contract and disclosure documents are complete and reviewed by your conveyancer** before your first open home.
- [ ] **Cross-check the contract against your own knowledge of the property** — flag any inconsistency with your conveyancer immediately.
- [ ] **Keep a copy of everything provided to buyers.**

---

## Quick Reference — ACT Vendor Disclosure Essentials

| Requirement | Key detail |
|---|---|
| Contract preparation | Conveyancer/solicitor, before marketing begins |
| Land tenure | Leasehold (Crown lease), disclosure should reflect this |
| Owners corporation records | Required if applicable — gather early |
| Buyer stamp duty exemption | May apply broadly since 1 July 2026 — relevant to buyer demand |
