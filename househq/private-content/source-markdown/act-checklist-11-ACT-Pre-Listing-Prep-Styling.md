# Pre-Listing Prep & Styling Checklist — ACT
### Getting your property market-ready without overspending on the wrong things.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal, financial or valuation advice.*

---

## Section 1 — Before You Spend Anything

- [ ] **Engage your conveyancer or solicitor early** to prepare your contract and disclosure documents.
- [ ] **Get a professional appraisal from 2–3 agents** before deciding your preparation budget.
- [ ] **Decide: renovate, style, or sell as-is?** Major renovations rarely return their cost dollar-for-dollar in a short pre-sale timeframe.

## Section 2 — High-Return, Low-Cost Improvements

- [ ] **Declutter aggressively** — consistently the highest-return, lowest-cost step.
- [ ] **Deep clean throughout**, including often-overlooked areas.
- [ ] **Fix small, visible defects** — leaking taps, cracked tiles, chipped paint.
- [ ] **Freshen paint in neutral tones** where walls are marked or dated.
- [ ] **Service and clean heating systems before listing** — genuinely important in Canberra, where buyers inspecting through winter will notice and value functioning, efficient heating.
- [ ] **Improve street appeal.**

## Section 3 — Styling Decisions

- [ ] **Consider professional staging for vacant properties.**
- [ ] **For occupied properties, focus on de-personalising** rather than full staging.
- [ ] **Get professional photography and floor plans regardless of styling budget** — not an area to cut costs on.
- [ ] **Ask your agent for their honest opinion on styling ROI** for your specific property.

## Section 4 — Compliance Items to Check Before Listing

- [ ] **Check smoke alarm compliance.**
- [ ] **If your home is older, consider whether any historical asbestos assessment is relevant** — Canberra has a well-documented history with loose-fill asbestos insulation in some older homes, and buyers or their conveyancers may specifically ask about this.
- [ ] **If in a unit-titled scheme, gather your records** — recent meeting minutes, financials, and any known disputes or special levies.

## Section 5 — Timing Your Campaign

- [ ] **Discuss timing with your agent relative to market conditions and Canberra's seasonal patterns**, including the significant influence of public service employment cycles on buyer activity.
- [ ] **Align your preparation timeline with your agent's proposed campaign start.**
- [ ] **Consider highlighting your property's eligibility for the new stamp duty exemption** if it's a new unit-titled property — this could be a genuine selling point given the recent reform.
- [ ] **Plan your own moving logistics in parallel** — see the separate Pre-Settlement Checklist in this series.

---

## Quick Reference — Where to Spend, Where to Save

| High-return, low-cost | Often lower-return relative to cost |
|---|---|
| Decluttering | Full kitchen/bathroom renovations |
| Deep cleaning | High-end fixture upgrades |
| Minor repairs | Structural changes shortly before sale |
| Professional photography | Extensive landscaping overhauls |
| Heating system servicing | Full furniture replacement for occupied homes |
