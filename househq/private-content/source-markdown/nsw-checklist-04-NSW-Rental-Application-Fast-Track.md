# Rental Application Fast-Track Checklist — New South Wales
### Win the lease, even with no rental history or connections.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal advice. NSW's rental laws changed significantly through 2024–2026 — this checklist reflects the current framework.*

---

## Section 1 — Know Your Rights Before You Apply

- [ ] **Know you can't be charged extra fees to apply.** Since 31 October 2024, tenants and prospective tenants cannot be charged for costs like background checks or fees for preparing a tenancy agreement — if you're asked to pay to apply, that's not permitted.
- [ ] **Understand the standard form agreement.** NSW tenancy agreements must use the standard form prescribed under Schedule 1 of the Residential Tenancies Regulation 2019 (current version from 19 May 2025) — this sets out mandatory terms that can't be varied to your disadvantage.
- [ ] **Know your protection against no-grounds evictions from day one.** Since 19 May 2025, landlords cannot end a tenancy without a valid, documented reason — this applies from the very start of your tenancy, giving you more long-term security than in the past.

## Section 2 — Documents to Have Ready

- [ ] **Photo ID** (driver's licence or passport).
- [ ] **Proof of income** — payslips, an employment letter, or for students/first-time renters: Centrelink statements, scholarship confirmation, or a guarantor's supporting documents.
- [ ] **Rental history/references** — previous landlord/agent details, or alternative references (employer, academic, personal) if you're a first-time renter.
- [ ] **Bank statements**, if requested, showing regular income/savings patterns.
- [ ] **A brief introduction** outlining your situation and rental intentions — not mandatory, but can help you stand out where agents allow supplementary information.

## Section 3 — The Application Itself

- [ ] **Apply promptly after inspecting** — competitive Sydney properties can attract many applications within the first day or two.
- [ ] **Only pay fees that are genuinely permitted** — remember, application/background-check fees are banned; if in doubt, ask NSW Fair Trading.
- [ ] **Attend the inspection in person if possible** — a strong signal of genuine interest.
- [ ] **Follow up politely, once**, a few days after applying if you haven't heard back.

## Section 4 — Renting With Pets (A Major 2025 Change)

- [ ] **Know that landlords can no longer issue blanket pet refusals.** Since May 2025, landlords must provide approval or a reason from a defined list of valid grounds when refusing a pet request.
- [ ] **Understand the 21-day response rule.** If a landlord doesn't respond to your written pet request (via the NSW Fair Trading Pet Application Form, Part A) within **21 days**, the request is **automatically approved**.
- [ ] **Know landlords cannot charge extra rent or bond because you have a pet.**
- [ ] **Assistance animals are protected separately** under federal disability discrimination law and don't require landlord consent.

## Section 5 — For Students & First-Time Renters Specifically

- [ ] **Consider a guarantor** if you don't have independent rental/income history — confirm the specific agency genuinely accepts this before assuming it will help your case.
- [ ] **Highlight stability signals** — enrolment confirmation, course length, stable casual work.
- [ ] **Consider co-applying with reliable housemates** for a combined, stronger application.
- [ ] **Don't be discouraged by rejections** — apply for multiple properties in parallel in a competitive market like Sydney.
