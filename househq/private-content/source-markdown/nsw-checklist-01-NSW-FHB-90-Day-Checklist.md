# The First Home Buyer's 90-Day Checklist — New South Wales
### Everything you need to sort before you sign, in plain English.

*Correct as at July 2026. Grant amounts, price caps and eligibility rules are set by government and can change — always confirm current details with Revenue NSW (revenue.nsw.gov.au) or your lender before relying on this for a purchase decision. This checklist is general information, not financial or legal advice.*

---

## Section 1 — Get Your Foundations Right (Do this first, before house-hunting)

- [ ] **Check your borrowing capacity with 2–3 lenders or a mortgage broker** — borrowing power varies more between lenders than most people expect.
- [ ] **Check if you qualify for the federal First Home Guarantee.** Places are currently unlimited with no income caps. Property price caps apply and differ between Sydney and regional NSW — confirm the current caps with your lender, as these are reviewed periodically.
- [ ] **Understand the First Home Buyers Assistance Scheme (FHBAS) — your biggest likely saving.** This gives a **full transfer duty (stamp duty) exemption** on new or established homes valued **up to $800,000**, and a **sliding-scale concession** for properties between **$800,000 and $1,000,000**. Above $1 million, no exemption or concession applies. On an $800,000 home, this saves roughly $31,000 in duty that would otherwise apply.
- [ ] **Check your eligibility for the First Home Owner (New Homes) Grant.** This is a **one-off $10,000 payment**, but only for **new homes** (never previously occupied or sold). Price caps apply: the total purchase price must not exceed **$600,000** for a new home purchase, or the combined land + building contract value must not exceed **$750,000** if building. This is separate from, and stacks with, the FHBAS duty exemption/concession.
- [ ] **Watch the spouse/partner rule.** If your spouse or partner has ever owned residential property in Australia — even if they're not on this title — you may be ineligible for the FHOG and FHBAS. This catches people out regularly, so check this early.
- [ ] **Understand the residence requirement.** You must move into the property within 12 months of settlement and live there continuously for at least 12 months to keep these benefits — failing this can require repayment plus penalties.
- [ ] **Consider house-and-land package structuring.** If you use separate contracts for land and building, stamp duty applies only to the land component — this can bring you under the FHBAS threshold even where the total package would otherwise exceed it. Make sure your solicitor structures this correctly.
- [ ] **Understand the Help to Buy shared equity scheme** — a federal scheme where government can co-invest, reducing what you need to borrow. Note: you can't use Help to Buy and the First Home Guarantee 5% deposit scheme at the same time — compare which gives the better outcome for your situation.
- [ ] **Get formal pre-approval, not just an estimate** — this strengthens your negotiating position and confirms your real, lender-verified budget.

## Section 2 — While You're Searching

- [ ] **Set a firm maximum price** based on your pre-approval.
- [ ] **Budget for costs beyond the purchase price**: legal/conveyancing fees, building and pest inspections, loan fees, and moving costs — even with $0 stamp duty, these add up to real money.
- [ ] **Note that in NSW, the vendor prepares the Contract of Sale before listing** — meaning you (or your solicitor) can and should request and review this early, before you're deep into negotiations.

## Section 3 — Know Your Legal Protections Before You Sign

- [ ] **Understand the cooling-off period.** For private treaty sales in NSW, you get **5 business days from exchange of contracts** to withdraw. Auctions have **no cooling-off period** at all, and this also applies if you exchange within 5 business days of a scheduled auction for that property.
- [ ] **Know the cost of cooling off.** If you withdraw during the cooling-off period, you forfeit **0.25% of the purchase price** to the vendor.
- [ ] **Get a building and pest inspection done before exchange (private treaty) or before auction day.** There is no cooling-off period at auction, so this is your only real opportunity to catch serious issues beforehand.
- [ ] **Have a solicitor or conveyancer review the Contract of Sale before you sign** — including the s10.7 planning certificate (formerly known as the s149 certificate), title details, and any special conditions.

## Section 4 — Between Signing and Settlement

- [ ] **Confirm when insurance responsibility passes to you** — this can vary by contract terms in NSW, so confirm the exact timing with your solicitor and arrange building insurance accordingly.
- [ ] **Finalise unconditional loan approval** well before settlement.
- [ ] **Book a pre-settlement inspection** in the days before settlement.
- [ ] **Confirm who's lodging your FHOG/FHBAS paperwork** — usually your solicitor/conveyancer as part of settlement via Revenue NSW's combined application process, but confirm this explicitly.

## Section 5 — Common First-Home-Buyer Mistakes to Avoid

- [ ] **Don't assume the FHOG applies to established homes** — it's new homes only; the FHBAS duty exemption is the one that applies to established homes too.
- [ ] **Don't forget the spouse/partner ownership rule** — this disqualifies many buyers who assume they're eligible.
- [ ] **Don't skip the inspection to save money or move fast** — especially critical given there's no cooling-off period at auction.
- [ ] **Don't exceed the price caps by a small margin without checking first** — going even $1 over the $800,000 FHBAS exemption threshold moves you into the concessional (not free) bracket, and going over $1 million removes the benefit entirely.

## Quick Reference — NSW First Home Buyer Support (July 2026)

| Support | Amount / Detail | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| FHBAS full duty exemption | $0 stamp duty | New or established homes, ≤$800,000 |
| FHBAS concession | Sliding scale | $800,000–$1,000,000 |
| First Home Owner (New Homes) Grant | $10,000 one-off | New homes only, ≤$600,000 (or ≤$750,000 land+build) |
| First Home Guarantee (federal) | 5% deposit, no LMI | Price caps vary Sydney vs. regional NSW |
| Cooling-off period | 5 business days | Private treaty only, not auctions; 0.25% penalty to withdraw |

*Sources: Revenue NSW (revenue.nsw.gov.au), NSW Government. Always verify current figures before making a purchase decision.*
