# Pre-Settlement to Moving Day Checklist — New South Wales
### The 30–90 day logistics, so nothing falls through the cracks.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal or financial advice.*

---

## Section 1 — Immediately After Exchange

- [ ] **Confirm building insurance timing with your solicitor.** Risk generally passes to the buyer at exchange in NSW under standard contract terms, but confirm this explicitly and arrange cover promptly rather than assuming settlement day is the trigger.
- [ ] **Send your signed contract to your lender/broker immediately** so formal loan documents can be prepared.
- [ ] **Diarise your cooling-off deadline** if it applies (5 business days for private treaty sales).
- [ ] **Engage your solicitor/conveyancer formally** so they can proceed with searches, requisitions, and liaison with the vendor's side.

## Section 2 — 4–6 Weeks Before Settlement

- [ ] **Finalise unconditional loan approval** and satisfy all outstanding lender conditions.
- [ ] **Get at least 3 removalist quotes** — book early, especially over summer and end-of-month periods when demand peaks.
- [ ] **Start decluttering and sourcing packing materials.**
- [ ] **Notify your current landlord/agent (if renting)** of your move-out date, respecting your required notice period.

## Section 3 — 1–2 Weeks Before Settlement

- [ ] **Organise utility connections for the new property** — electricity, gas, water, internet/NBN (prioritise NBN given longer typical connection lead times).
- [ ] **Arrange disconnection at your current address** for the day after you vacate.
- [ ] **Redirect your mail** via Australia Post (available for up to 12 months).
- [ ] **Update your address** with: the electoral roll, your bank(s), employer, Medicare, insurer(s), and Service NSW (driver's licence and vehicle registration).
- [ ] **Confirm final settlement figures** with your solicitor/lender, including rates and any strata levy adjustments.
- [ ] **Confirm who's lodging your FHOG/FHBAS paperwork**, if applicable, and that it's genuinely underway.

## Section 4 — Settlement Week

- [ ] **Book your pre-settlement inspection** in the days before settlement to confirm the property matches the agreed condition and inclusions.
- [ ] **Confirm final funds transfer arrangements** with your solicitor and lender.
- [ ] **Confirm key collection arrangements** — where and when you'll receive keys once settlement is confirmed complete.
- [ ] **Confirm your removalist booking for the day after settlement** (or settlement day itself if timing allows) — don't schedule movers before settlement is legally confirmed.

## Section 5 — Moving Day and Just After

- [ ] **Do a final walkthrough before unpacking**, photographing anything that wasn't present at your pre-settlement inspection.
- [ ] **Locate your fuse box, water mains shut-off, and gas shut-off** early.
- [ ] **Set up home and contents insurance** if not already arranged — don't rely on the vendor's cover, which typically ends at settlement.
- [ ] **Register relevant council services** (bin collection, etc.) if not automatically transferred.
- [ ] **Keep all settlement paperwork** (contract, s10.7 certificate, loan documents, FHOG/FHBAS confirmation) together — you'll need these for tax, insurance, and future reference.

## Quick Reference — Settlement Week Timeline

| Timing | Key task |
|---|---|
| Exchange of contracts | Confirm insurance timing, engage solicitor |
| 4–6 weeks out | Loan approval finalised, book removalist |
| 1–2 weeks out | Utilities connected, mail redirected, address updates |
| Settlement week | Pre-settlement inspection, confirm funds transfer |
| Settlement day | Keys released, final walkthrough |
