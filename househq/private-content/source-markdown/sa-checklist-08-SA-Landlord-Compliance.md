# Landlord Compliance Checklist — South Australia
### The obligations every rental provider must meet under the 2024–2026 reforms.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal advice. Confirm current requirements with Consumer and Business Services (CBS) SA before relying on this checklist alone.*

---

## Section 1 — Using the Correct Prescribed Forms

- [ ] **Use current prescribed forms** — these changed on 1 July 2024 and again on 1 September 2025 under the Residential Tenancies Regulations 2025. Forms from before these dates may no longer be compliant.
- [ ] **Use the correct termination form for your specific ground** — SA has six landlord termination forms (Forms 5–10), and using the wrong one for your situation can invalidate the notice entirely.

## Section 2 — Ending a Tenancy

- [ ] **Know no-grounds terminations were abolished from 1 July 2024.** Every termination notice must state a prescribed reason.
- [ ] **Know the notice period rules for fixed-term tenancies.** Outside specific grounds (breach under Form 5, or drug contamination under Form 6), a fixed-term tenancy can generally only be terminated at the end of its term, with at least **60 days' notice** on a prescribed ground.
- [ ] **Know about the mandatory evidence requirement.** For possession-based grounds (demolition, renovation, family occupation, sale, sales agency), you need written evidence approved by the Commissioner — a stated intention alone isn't sufficient.
- [ ] **Understand the 6-month re-letting restriction.** After terminating on any of the 5 possession-based grounds, you generally cannot re-let the property for 6 months, whether the tenancy was periodic or fixed-term, unless SACAT grants specific consent.
- [ ] **Follow the correct rent arrears process.** A 7-day remedy period generally applies following a Form 5 breach notice — if the tenant pays within that window, the notice has no further effect.

## Section 3 — Rent and Bonds

- [ ] **Increase rent no more than once every 12 months**, with at least 60 days' written notice.
- [ ] **Lodge bonds within the required deadline** — 2 weeks if you're self-managing, 4 weeks if you're using a registered agent.
- [ ] **Never claim fair wear and tear against a bond.**
- [ ] **Meet the stricter evidence requirements** for any bond claim you make, introduced as part of the 2024 reforms.

## Section 4 — Pets

- [ ] **Understand pets are now permitted subject to your approval and reasonable conditions** — since 1 July 2024, you can no longer issue a blanket refusal; you can require reasonable conditions (e.g. the pet staying outside, or professional carpet cleaning at lease end) but must genuinely consider requests.

## Section 5 — Penalties and Disputes

- [ ] **Understand SACAT's role** in resolving tenancy disputes, including possession, bond, and compensation matters.
- [ ] **Know about the internal review limit for money orders.** Since the 2024 reforms, a party generally cannot seek internal review of a SACAT order requiring payment of money except in exceptional circumstances — designed to prevent payment delays, but meaning you should treat your SACAT hearing as your primary opportunity to present your case.
- [ ] **Keep thorough records** — rent payments, repair requests and responses, condition reports, and all tenant communications.
