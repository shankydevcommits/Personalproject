# Landlord Compliance Checklist — New South Wales
### The obligations every rental provider must meet under the 2024–2026 reforms.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal advice. Confirm current requirements with NSW Fair Trading before relying on this checklist alone.*

---

## Section 1 — The Standard Form Agreement

- [ ] **Use the mandatory standard form agreement** — prescribed under Schedule 1 of the Residential Tenancies Regulation 2019, current version from 19 May 2025 (last updated 17 December 2025). Using a non-compliant agreement risks unenforceability at NCAT and penalties from NSW Fair Trading.
- [ ] **Complete the Schedule 2 condition report** at the start of every tenancy and keep a copy.
- [ ] **Never charge application, background-check, or agreement-preparation fees to prospective tenants** — banned since 31 October 2024.

## Section 2 — Ending a Tenancy

- [ ] **Know no-grounds evictions are abolished.** Since 19 May 2025, you must have a valid, documented reason to end any tenancy (fixed-term or periodic) — e.g. sale requiring vacant possession, significant renovation/demolition, the landlord or family member moving in, or a genuine tenant breach.
- [ ] **Provide a Termination Information Statement** with supporting evidence when ending a tenancy — it's an offence to give a termination notice on a ground that isn't genuine, or with false/misleading supporting documents.
- [ ] **Follow the rent arrears process correctly**: written breach notice on day 14 of arrears, followed by a termination notice if unpaid, then an NCAT application for possession if the breach isn't remedied.
- [ ] **Provide additional documentation for renovation-based terminations** — required since 20 June 2025, beyond simply stating an intention to renovate.
- [ ] **Complete the mandatory end-of-tenancy survey** in Rental Bonds Online within 14 days of a bond claim, identifying who ended the tenancy and why — non-compliance can result in a penalty notice from NSW Fair Trading.

## Section 3 — Rent and Pets

- [ ] **Increase rent no more than once every 12 months**, with at least **60 days' written notice**.
- [ ] **Respond to pet requests within 21 days** (via the Fair Trading Pet Application Form) or the request is automatically approved — you can only refuse for reasons from the prescribed list, not a blanket policy.
- [ ] **Never charge extra rent or bond because a tenant has a pet.**
- [ ] **Offer Centrepay as a payment option for eligible tenants** — mandatory since 2 March 2026.

## Section 4 — Ongoing Obligations

- [ ] **Ensure smoke alarms are installed and maintained in working order** — confirm current specific testing/replacement requirements with NSW Fair Trading, as these are periodically updated.
- [ ] **Respond to repair requests promptly**, particularly anything affecting habitability — unresolved requests can escalate to NCAT.
- [ ] **Lodge and manage bonds only through Rental Bonds Online** — landlords/agents don't hold bond funds directly.
- [ ] **Keep records of all tenancy communications** — correspondence, notices, and evidence supporting any termination ground.

## Section 5 — Penalties for Non-Compliance

- [ ] **Understand NCAT's powers** — the Tribunal can make binding orders on possession, compensation, and bond disputes, and can be applied to by either party.
- [ ] **Know NSW Fair Trading can issue penalty notices** for specific breaches, including failure to complete the mandatory end-of-tenancy survey.
- [ ] **Understand it's an offence to issue a termination notice on a non-genuine ground** or with false supporting documents — this carries real legal exposure beyond a simple compliance slip-up.
