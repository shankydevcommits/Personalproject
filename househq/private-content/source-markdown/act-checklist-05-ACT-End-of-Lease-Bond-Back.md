# End-of-Lease Bond-Back Checklist — ACT
### Protect your money — most bond disputes are avoidable with the right documentation.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal advice.*

---

## Section 1 — Understand How Your Bond Works

- [ ] **Know your bond is lodged with the ACT Revenue Office**, not held by your landlord or agent directly — lessors must lodge within 2 weeks of receiving it, agents within 4 weeks.
- [ ] **Know the maximum bond is 4 weeks' rent.**
- [ ] **Understand bond is not a general maintenance fund** — it can only be claimed against for unpaid rent, cleaning to restore the property (allowing for fair wear and tear), or damage beyond fair wear and tear.

## Section 2 — Before You Move Out

- [ ] **Locate your original entry condition report** — your baseline for comparison.
- [ ] **Compare the property's current condition against that report**, room by room.
- [ ] **Clean thoroughly**, addressing anything genuinely beyond fair wear and tear.
- [ ] **Check every item on the original inventory** is present and in working order.
- [ ] **Settle any outstanding rent** before vacating.

## Section 3 — At the Final Inspection

- [ ] **Attend the final inspection if you can**, comparing the property against the original condition report.
- [ ] **Take your own timestamped photos** of every room after cleaning, regardless of whether the agent attends.
- [ ] **Keep every receipt** — cleaning, repairs, pest control — as evidence.

## Section 4 — Claiming Your Bond

- [ ] **Use the Rental Bond Refund form** if all parties agree — the ACT Revenue Office can then release the bond according to the agreed split.
- [ ] **If there's a dispute**, either party can apply to the **ACT Civil and Administrative Tribunal (ACAT)** for resolution.
- [ ] **Know ACAT can order the bond released in whole or part** to one or more parties after hearing evidence from both sides.

## Section 5 — If There's a Dispute

- [ ] **Gather your evidence before applying to ACAT**: entry and exit condition reports, timestamped photos, receipts, and correspondence with your landlord/agent.
- [ ] **Apply promptly and include your evidence with your application** — ACAT sets specific time limits and application steps, so don't delay once a dispute is clear.
- [ ] **Bring copies of any bond forms you or the landlord have signed**, and note the dates you submitted anything relevant.

---

## Quick Reference — ACT Bond Process

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Bond holder | ACT Revenue Office |
| Maximum bond | 4 weeks' rent |
| Lodgement deadline | 2 weeks (lessor) / 4 weeks (agent) |
| Dispute resolution | ACAT (ACT Civil and Administrative Tribunal) |
| Fair wear and tear | Cannot be charged against your bond |
