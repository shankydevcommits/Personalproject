# Pre-Listing Prep & Styling Checklist — Queensland
### Getting your property market-ready without overspending on the wrong things.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal, financial or valuation advice.*

---

## Section 1 — Before You Spend Anything

- [ ] **Start your Seller Disclosure Statement and certificates early.** Since this is now mandatory before a buyer can sign (since 1 August 2025), engage your solicitor or conveyancer as one of your very first steps, well before your first open home.
- [ ] **Get a professional appraisal from 2–3 agents** before deciding your preparation budget.
- [ ] **Decide: renovate, style, or sell as-is?** Major renovations rarely return their cost dollar-for-dollar in a short pre-sale timeframe.

## Section 2 — High-Return, Low-Cost Improvements

- [ ] **Declutter aggressively** — consistently the highest-return, lowest-cost step.
- [ ] **Deep clean throughout**, paying particular attention to mould and mildew given Queensland's humidity — visible mould is a red flag that costs you buyer confidence fast.
- [ ] **Fix small, visible defects** — leaking taps, cracked tiles, chipped paint, and anything storm/water-damage related.
- [ ] **Freshen paint in neutral tones** where walls are marked or dated.
- [ ] **Improve street appeal** — including tidying tropical gardens/lawns, which can grow quickly and look unkempt fast in Queensland's climate if neglected pre-listing.

## Section 3 — Styling Decisions

- [ ] **Consider professional staging for vacant properties.**
- [ ] **For occupied properties, focus on de-personalising** rather than full staging.
- [ ] **Get professional photography and floor plans regardless of styling budget** — not an area to cut costs on.
- [ ] **Ask your agent for their honest opinion on styling ROI** for your specific property.

## Section 4 — Compliance and Disclosure Items to Check Before Listing

- [ ] **Prepare your Seller Disclosure Statement (Form 2) and prescribed certificates** — this is now a prerequisite, not an optional nicety, and delays here can hold up your whole campaign.
- [ ] **Check smoke alarm compliance.**
- [ ] **If in a body corporate scheme, gather your records** — recent AGM minutes, financials, and any known disputes or special levies, which buyers' solicitors will request as part of their own due diligence.
- [ ] **Address any known structural, water damage, or pest issues honestly** — under the new disclosure regime, undisclosed material matters can now give a buyer grounds to walk away and reclaim their deposit, so it's genuinely in your interest to be upfront rather than hope an issue goes unnoticed.

## Section 5 — Timing Your Campaign

- [ ] **Discuss timing with your agent relative to market conditions and Queensland's seasonal patterns.**
- [ ] **Align your preparation and disclosure timeline with your agent's proposed campaign start** — given the new disclosure requirement, this now needs more lead time than Queensland sellers may be used to from before August 2025.
- [ ] **Plan your own moving logistics in parallel** — see the separate Pre-Settlement Checklist in this series.

---

## Quick Reference — Where to Spend, Where to Save

| High-return, low-cost | Often lower-return relative to cost |
|---|---|
| Decluttering | Full kitchen/bathroom renovations |
| Deep cleaning (esp. mould/mildew) | High-end fixture upgrades |
| Minor repairs | Structural changes shortly before sale |
| Professional photography | Extensive landscaping overhauls |
| Fresh neutral paint (where needed) | Full furniture replacement for occupied homes |
