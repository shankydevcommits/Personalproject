# Rental Application Fast-Track Checklist — Tasmania
### Win the lease, even with no rental history or connections.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal advice.*

---

## Section 1 — Know Your Rights Before You Apply

- [ ] **Know your tenancy is governed by the Residential Tenancy Act 1997 (Tas)**, consolidated with reforms through to at least 20 March 2026.
- [ ] **Understand Tasmania's disputes process is different from most other states.** Most tenancy matters — bonds, rent, repairs, termination — are handled first by the **Residential Tenancy Commissioner**, and where needed, the **Magistrates Court**, rather than a specialist tenancy tribunal like NCAT, QCAT, or SACAT. There is one narrow exception: since 20 March 2026, the **Tasmanian Civil and Administrative Tribunal (TASCAT)** hears **pet-consent disputes only**.

## Section 2 — Documents to Have Ready

- [ ] **Photo ID** (driver's licence or passport).
- [ ] **Proof of income** — payslips, employment letter, or for students/first-time renters: Centrelink statements, scholarship confirmation, or guarantor documents.
- [ ] **Rental history/references** — previous landlord/agent details, or alternative references if you're a first-time renter.
- [ ] **Bank statements**, if requested.

## Section 3 — The Application Itself

- [ ] **Apply promptly after inspecting.**
- [ ] **Attend the inspection in person if possible.**
- [ ] **Follow up politely, once**, if you haven't heard back within a few days.

## Section 4 — Renting With Pets (A New Process Since March 2026)

- [ ] **Know pets require landlord consent by default in Tasmania** — there's no automatic right to keep a pet the way some other states now provide.
- [ ] **Know a blanket "no pets" refusal isn't compliant.** A lease can't impose a blanket no-pets condition where doing so would amount to an unreasonable refusal.
- [ ] **Know where to dispute a refusal.** Since 20 March 2026, pet-consent disputes specifically go to **TASCAT** under the Residential Tenancy Amendment (Pets) Act 2025 — a dedicated pathway separate from other tenancy matters.
- [ ] **Know assistance animals are protected separately** under the Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Cth) regardless of lease terms.

## Section 5 — For Students & First-Time Renters Specifically

- [ ] **Consider a guarantor** if you lack independent rental/income history.
- [ ] **Highlight stability signals** — enrolment confirmation, course length, stable casual work history.
- [ ] **Consider co-applying with reliable housemates** for a stronger combined application.
- [ ] **Don't be discouraged by rejections** — apply for multiple properties in parallel.
