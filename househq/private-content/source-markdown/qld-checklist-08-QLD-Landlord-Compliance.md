# Landlord Compliance Checklist — Queensland
### The obligations every rental provider must meet under the RTRA Act and 2024–2026 reforms.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal advice. Confirm current requirements with the RTA before relying on this checklist alone.*

---

## Section 1 — Minimum Housing Standards

Since 1 September 2024, all rental properties in Queensland must:

- [ ] **Be weatherproof, structurally sound, and free from mould and vermin.**
- [ ] **Have functioning locks** on external doors and windows where reasonable.
- [ ] **Have adequate plumbing.**
- [ ] **Provide privacy window coverings.**
- [ ] **Provide safe fixtures and fittings.**
- [ ] **Provide a functional kitchen and laundry**, where these are supplied as part of the tenancy.
- [ ] **Note the RTA's practical approach for older properties** — fixtures that are safe and functional, even if outdated, are generally accepted; the standard is about safety and function, not modernisation.

## Section 2 — Applications and Privacy

- [ ] **Use the standardised rental application form (Form 22)**, mandatory since May 2025.
- [ ] **Follow the new rules on personal information** — collection, storage, and destruction requirements were tightened alongside the Form 22 changes.

## Section 3 — Rent and Ending Tenancies

- [ ] **Increase rent no more than once every 12 months**, and remember this limit is tied to the **property itself** (since 6 June 2024), not the individual tenancy — you can't reset the clock by ending and re-letting.
- [ ] **Know no-grounds evictions have been banned since 1 October 2022.** You need a valid reason: sale requiring vacant possession, significant renovation, owner/family member moving in, or a genuine breach.
- [ ] **Provide correct notice periods** for the specific ground you're using to end a tenancy.
- [ ] **Complete the formal notice process for rent arrears**: this generally follows a structured breach notice and remedy period before any QCAT application for possession.

## Section 4 — Bonds

- [ ] **Never request more than 4 weeks' rent as bond.**
- [ ] **Lodge bonds with the RTA** — you don't hold bond funds directly.
- [ ] **Complete entry (Form 1a) and exit (Form 14a) condition reports** for every tenancy.
- [ ] **Be aware of the portable bond pilot** in South East Queensland (from January 2026) if your outgoing/incoming tenants are eligible.

## Section 5 — Pets and Household Circumstances

- [ ] **Respond to pet requests within 14 days**, or consent is automatically deemed granted.
- [ ] **Only refuse on reasonable grounds** — a blanket policy isn't compliant.
- [ ] **Understand the co-renter protection for family violence situations** — since 6 June 2024, a co-renter can apply to QCAT to be recognised as the sole renter where a household member is violent, which may affect your tenancy agreement independent of the original arrangement.

## Section 6 — Penalties and Disputes

- [ ] **Understand QCAT's powers** — repair orders, compensation, possession orders, and bond dispute resolution all sit with QCAT.
- [ ] **Keep thorough records** — rent payments, repair requests and responses, condition reports, and all tenant communications, since QCAT decisions rely heavily on documented evidence.
