# Contract & Building/Pest Inspection Red Flags — Tasmania
### What to check before you sign — because in Tasmania, there's no cooling off to fall back on.

*Correct as at July 2026. General information only, not legal advice — always have a conveyancer or solicitor review your specific contract before signing.*

---

## Section 1 — The Single Most Important Thing to Understand About TAS Contracts

- [ ] **There is no cooling-off period in Tasmania**, similar to Western Australia. Once both parties sign the contract of sale, you are bound — there is no statutory right to withdraw.
- [ ] **Every condition you need must be written into the contract before you sign** — finance approval, building/pest inspection, or anything else. There's no fallback protection once you've signed.

## Section 2 — Building & Pest Inspection: Even More Critical Here

- [ ] **Arrange your building and pest inspection before you sign**, or ensure the contract includes a clearly worded, time-limited inspection condition.
- [ ] **Use a licensed, independent inspector.**
- [ ] **Get both a building and pest inspection**, ideally combined.
- [ ] **Ask specifically about**: structural issues, rising damp (a genuine concern in Tasmania's cooler, wetter climate), roof condition, asbestos (common in pre-1990s homes), and general electrical/plumbing age.
- [ ] **Get the report in writing before you sign**, not after.

## Section 3 — Contract Fundamentals

- [ ] **Check the finance clause wording carefully** — if you're not fully pre-approved, ensure a genuine, clearly time-limited finance condition is included.
- [ ] **Confirm the settlement date** works with your finance, insurance, and moving logistics.
- [ ] **Confirm deposit terms** — amount, due date, and where it's held.
- [ ] **Confirm all expected inclusions are explicitly listed** in the contract.
- [ ] **If buying a strata property, review the strata records** — recent meeting minutes, financials, sinking fund balance, and any known disputes or special levies.

## Section 4 — Before You Sign: Final Checks

- [ ] **Have a conveyancer or solicitor review the entire contract before you sign it** — not after. This is your primary protection given the absence of a cooling-off period.
- [ ] **Confirm current stamp duty and grant settings apply to your specific purchase before you finalise your budget** — given the significant policy transition around 30 June 2026 (see Checklist #1 in this series), don't assume figures you've seen elsewhere still apply.
- [ ] **Don't let time pressure push you into signing before your review and inspection are genuinely complete.**

## Quick Reference — Key TAS Contract Realities

| Protection | What it means |
|---|---|
| Cooling-off period | **None** — you're bound the moment both parties sign |
| Finance/inspection conditions | Must be explicitly written into the contract before signing |
| Building/pest inspection | Not automatically provided — arrange your own before signing |
| Contract review | Must happen before signature, not after |
