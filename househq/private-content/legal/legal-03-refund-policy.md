## Your Rights Under Australian Consumer Law

Nothing in this policy limits or excludes your rights under the **Australian Consumer Law (ACL)**. Our Checklists come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure, and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have a Checklist replaced or fixed if it fails to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

## Faulty or Inaccessible Files

If your Checklist file is corrupted, fails to download, or otherwise doesn't work as described, contact us at **[insert contact email]** and we will:
- Re-send a working copy of your file, or
- Provide a full refund, if a working copy cannot be provided within a reasonable time.

## Change of Mind

Because our Checklists are digital products delivered instantly upon verified purchase, **we generally do not offer refunds for change of mind** once a Checklist has been successfully downloaded. This is consistent with standard practice for digital goods under Australian Consumer Law, which does not require businesses to provide change-of-mind refunds (as distinct from the consumer guarantees described above, which always apply).

If you haven't yet downloaded your purchase and would like to request a change-of-mind refund, contact us within **48 hours** of purchase at **[insert contact email]** and we'll consider your request.

## Incorrect Purchase

If you accidentally purchased the wrong Checklist (e.g. the wrong state, or the wrong buyer/renter/investor/seller category) and have not yet downloaded it, contact us within **48 hours** and we will happily exchange it for the correct one at no extra cost.

## How to Request a Refund or Replacement

Email **[insert contact email]** with:
- Your order number or the email address used for purchase.
- The Checklist(s) involved.
- A brief description of the issue.

We aim to respond to all refund and replacement requests within **2 business days**.

## Bundles

If you purchased a bundle and only part of it is faulty or inaccessible, we will replace or refund the affected item(s) rather than the full bundle, unless the fault affects the bundle as a whole.
