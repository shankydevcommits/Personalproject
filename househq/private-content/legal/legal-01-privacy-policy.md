## 1. What We Collect

When you use HouseHQ, we may collect:
- **Contact information**: your name, email address, and phone number, provided when you create an account or purchase a checklist.
- **Verification data**: confirmation that your email address and/or phone number are valid and belong to you, collected as part of our download verification process (see Section 4).
- **Purchase information**: the products you buy, transaction amounts, and payment confirmation (note: we do not directly store your full card details — payment processing is handled by our payment provider, [Payhip/Stripe/etc.], in accordance with their own privacy and security standards).
- **Usage information**: pages viewed, checklists downloaded, and general interaction data with our website, collected via standard analytics tools.
- **Marketing preferences**: whether you've opted in to receive email or SMS marketing communications from us.

## 2. Why We Collect It

We collect and use your personal information to:
- Verify your identity and deliver the digital products you've purchased.
- Process payments and provide customer support.
- Send you transactional communications (purchase confirmations, download links).
- With your separate, explicit consent, send you marketing communications about new checklists, bundles, or related content.
- Improve our website and product offering.
- Meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

## 3. Marketing Consent

We only send marketing emails or SMS messages to people who have given **express, opt-in consent** to receive them, consistent with the *Spam Act 2003* (Cth). Consent for email marketing and SMS marketing is captured separately — ticking one box does not automatically opt you into the other channel. Every marketing message we send includes a clear, functional way to unsubscribe, and we action unsubscribe requests promptly.

## 4. Email Verification and Phone Number Collection

To access purchased checklists, we require you to verify your email address via a one-time code before your download unlocks. We also collect your mobile phone number at checkout for order-related contact and, where you've separately opted in, SMS marketing — but we do not currently verify phone numbers via one-time code. Providing your phone number is required to complete a purchase, consistent with the rest of our checkout process, but verification applies to your email address only.

## 5. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with:
- **Payment processors** (e.g. Payhip, Stripe) to complete transactions.
- **Email/SMS service providers** (e.g. Brevo, Mailchimp, Twilio) solely to deliver transactional and, where consented, marketing communications.
- **Regulators or authorities**, where required by law.

We do not share your information with real estate agents, brokers, or other third parties for their own marketing purposes without your separate, explicit consent.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access, including secure storage and limited access to personal data within our business. No online system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to promptly addressing any data breach in accordance with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme under the Privacy Act.

## 7. Your Rights

- Ask what personal information we hold about you.
- Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
- Withdraw marketing consent at any time (via the unsubscribe link in any marketing message, or by contacting us directly).
- Ask us to delete your personal information, subject to any legal obligations we have to retain certain records (e.g. transaction records for tax purposes).
